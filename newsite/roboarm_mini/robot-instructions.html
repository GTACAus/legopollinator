Robo Pollinator Instructions

In this activity, you will control a robotic pollinator. Your goal is to pollinate as many flowers as you can in one minute.

Drag the robot to turn it. Tap ▶ Start for one minute, then + 1 flower each time you pollinate a flower.

What does the remote actually control?

03/05

Start the Robot Pollinator

Tap the numbers under the hub to go through each step. Drag the hub to turn it.

04/05

Connect your iPad to the robot

  1. On the iPad, open Settings → Wi-Fi.
  2. Join the network with your board's name, for example ROBOT 12. The password is gtacrobot.
  3. Open the Robot controller app on your iPad's home screen.

The iPad may say there is no internet on this Wi-Fi. That is fine: stay connected. Switch back to the Discovery Wi-Fi when you finish.

05/05

Drive your pollinator with the remote

  1. Make sure Basic is selected.
  2. Hold the D-pad (1) to drive: up and down drive forward and back, left and right turn.
  3. Push the slider (2) up or down to move the pollination arm.
  4. Tap ✎ Change remote to add, move or set up controls and choose which motor each one moves.

Want to code your robot instead? See Change the way your robot pollinator moves.
